About

SmoothDaddyG is a game studio.

We design and build our own games — every title is made in-house, from the first sketch to the final sound effect. Our designers, engineers, and playtesters obsess over one question: how simple can a game be while still being genuinely fun? How much can pictures, animation, and sound teach, so that reading never has to?

We make small, polished, arcade-inspired games for people who learn and play differently. Games are where we start, not where we end.

Our mission is building thoughtfully simple software.

A lot of modern software is complicated for no good reason. Menus inside menus, settings nobody asked for, dark patterns dressed up as design. We take the opposite approach: strip an idea down to its clearest form, make the first touch delightful, and respect the person holding the device.

SmoothDaddyG isn’t a therapy company, an education portal, or an accessibility consultancy. We’re a studio that ships software — software built with unusual care for the people most software leaves behind.

New cabinets drop regularly. The arcade is just getting warmed up.
